Knobhead16 is a macropad for knobheads (knob lovers, duh). It has a lot of knobs.

## Why?
Terrible naming meets unskilled engineering...and you get whatever this is.

Knobhead16 is a product development exercise. I was bored, had an unexpected surplus of time, and needed a macropad. So I thought: why not make one myself?

## Development stages
This project's development can be divided into 5 stages:

- **Sketching (Ergogen:)** Code-based layout/pcb/case creation tool. Used to define the key and knob footprints, sketch the pcb and get dimensions for a future case.
- **EDA (EasyEDA):** WebUI Electronics Design Automation tool. Used to trace and populate the pcb.
- **CAD (OnShape):** Designing the case, internal mcu mounts, switch plates and knobs.
- **Prototyping:** Assembling the macropad, fiddling with firmware and testing it.
- **Publishing:** Project files packaging and archive upload to github.

A [more in-depth devlog is available](https://www.typewatch.dev/knobhead16) should you want to follow along and make your own tweaks, or learn how to use the featured tools.

## Damn you, tariffs
I got from zero-to-prototype in about two weeks. If you add the waiting time for parts and supplies (I ran out of filament mid-progress D:), getting to a prototype took just shy of 2 months. Not bad, but not good either.
